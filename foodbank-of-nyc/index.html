For more than four decades we've stood with New Yorkers experiencing hunger. Our goal is simple: help every New Yorker reach lasting food security.

MEET NYC'S LARGEST HUNGER RELIEF ORGANIZATION

Working with close to 800 partner sites across all five boroughs, we provide fresh, culturally familiar food along with the resources people need to become self-sufficient.
