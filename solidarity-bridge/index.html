Increasing access to surgery

Donate

We believe that healthcare is a human right.

Working in solidarity, we team up with communities across Bolivia and Paraguay to expand access to safe, affordable surgical and essential health services.

An estimated 13 million people across these two countries still lack access to timely, affordable surgery.
