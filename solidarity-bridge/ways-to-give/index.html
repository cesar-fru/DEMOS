Your Gift Transforms Lives

Timely, essential surgical care changes lives and strengthens communities. It also opens the door to partnership and shared learning between visiting medical volunteers and local health professionals. Help us bring surgical care to those who need it most!

Legacy Gifting

Include Solidarity Bridge in your will or another charitable vehicle to help ensure surgical care remains available for generations of families and communities to come.

In-kind Donations Make an Impact

Medical Supplies

Surgical tools and supplies that are common in the U.S. remain out of reach for most people in Bolivia and Paraguay. Donated medical supplies directly strengthen the local hospitals and surgeons who care for our patients.

Before donating supplies, please check our Medical Supply Wish List first.

Other Donations

Beyond medical supplies, we're also grateful to accept:

  • Gently used luggage, which helps us transport supplies on medical trips.
  • Live and silent auction items — goods, services, or experiences around the Chicago area — for our annual Fiesta Gala, our biggest fundraiser of the year.

We Use Your Gift Responsibly

Solidarity Bridge is a registered 501(c)3 nonprofit, rated highly by GuideStar and Charity Navigator, and accredited by the Better Business Bureau. We direct 85% of donations straight to programs — well above the 65% average for national nonprofits — with the rest covering the operating costs that let us reach even more patients.
